Media kit

One line
Troy Meekhof is a Grand Rapids filmmaker, digital marketer, and Part 107 drone pilot, better known online as The Cybertruck Guy.

50 words
Troy Meekhof is a filmmaker and digital marketer in Grand Rapids, Michigan. He founded Mid-July Media, a wedding videography company, in 2019 and co-founded Grand Rapids Marketing Co. He is an FAA Part 107 drone pilot and, as The Cybertruck Guy, has been quoted in Newsweek on Cybertruck ownership.

150 words
Troy Meekhof is a filmmaker, digital marketer, and FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot based in Caledonia, Michigan, working the Grand Rapids market. He founded Mid-July Media in July 2019 and has filmed more than 200 Michigan weddings since 2017, every one as the videographer. He is Creative Director and Co-Founder of Grand Rapids Marketing Co., a no-nonsense agency for small and medium businesses. Before that he ran digital marketing at Treadstone Funding, where he grew organic search from just over 20 clicks a day to more than 300, and produced TV commercials and social video at Lake Michigan Credit Union. As The Cybertruck Guy (@cybrtrkguy), he documents real-life Cybertruck ownership for 26.8K followers on X and has been quoted in Newsweek and is an Evotrex brand ambassador. He filmed and produced the “Split the Mitten” documentary and holds a bachelor’s degree from Grand Valley State University (2020).
