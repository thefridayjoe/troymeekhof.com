About Troy Meekhof

The short version

Troy Meekhof (pronounced MEEK-hoff), also known as The Cybertruck Guy (@cybrtrkguy), is a filmmaker, digital marketer, and FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot based in Caledonia, Michigan, serving the Grand Rapids market. He is the founder and sole videographer of Mid-July Media, a Michigan wedding videography company founded in July 2019, and the Creative Director and Co-Founder of Grand Rapids Marketing Co., a digital marketing agency he co-founded with Sam Kiste. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Film/Cinema/Video Studies and Communication Studies from Grand Valley State University (2020). He has been quoted in Newsweek and Torque News on Cybertruck ownership, filmed and produced the “Split the Mitten” documentary covered by MLive, and spoke on stage at the Tesla Owners Club of Michigan Summer Meetup in June 2026.

That is the paragraph the search engines and AI models are looking for. The rest is for you.

The story

I have held a job since I was six. It started with a paper route, moved to mowing lawns, and by high school I was the school videographer at Calvin Christian and leading the cleaning crew after class. At 19 I filmed my first wedding. In July 2019 I made it a business and called it Mid-July Media, because that is when I started it and I am not a complicated person.

While building that, I worked full-time in marketing for two mortgage companies and a credit union. At Treadstone Funding I took the website from 50 clicks a day to 500 in two years. At Lake Michigan Credit Union I produced TV commercials, social video, and internal live streams for a team of 35-plus. In between, I co-founded Grand Rapids Marketing Co. with Sam Kiste to give small and medium businesses the same tools without the enterprise invoice.

Then, in early 2024, I took delivery of a Cybertruck and started writing about it. Newsweek called a year later. I did not plan for “The Cybertruck Guy” to be a job title. It is now.
