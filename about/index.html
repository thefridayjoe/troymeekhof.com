About Troy Meekhof

The story

I got my first job when I was 6.

It started with a paper route, moved to landscaping, and by 15 I was the school videographer, saving enough to buy my first car. In 2016 I became the shift supervisor at a local pet store, and in 2017 I filmed my first wedding, where I dove head first into the world of weddings and marketing.

While building that, I graduated from GVSU during the COVID-19 pandemic while simultaneously working full-time in marketing in finance.

At Treadstone Funding I took the website from 20 clicks a day to over 300 in two years. At Lake Michigan Credit Union I produced TV commercials, social media video, and internal live broadcasts for all 1,600 employees. In between, I co-founded Grand Rapids Marketing Co. with Sam Kiste to give small businesses the same tools without the enterprise invoice.

I started The Cybertruck Guy in 2019. In early 2024 I finally took delivery of a Cybertruck and started writing about it. Newsweek called a year later. I did not plan for “The Cybertruck Guy” to be a job title. It is now.
