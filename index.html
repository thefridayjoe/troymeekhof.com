About

West Michigan, born and stayed.

The long version →

I've held a job since I was six: a paper route, then mowing lawns, then the school videographer at Calvin Christian. At 19 I filmed my first wedding. In July 2019 I made it a business and called it Mid-July Media.

I studied Film/Video and Communication Studies at Grand Valley State University (class of 2020), and I'm an FAA Part 107 drone pilot. Home is the Village of Caledonia with my wife, our kid, and the dog.
