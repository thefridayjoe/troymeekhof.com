About

The long version →

I'm a local creative with advanced skills in video production, web development, GEO, SEO, and social media marketing. I'm also an EV enthusiast, a serial road-tripper, Part 107 drone pilot, and a sucker for cheap breakfast joints.

I've held a multitude of jobs since I was six years old — a paper route, mowing lawns — and soon found myself starting a media production company. That left me with a strong background in leadership, logistics, big visions, and meticulous attention to detail.

Grand Valley State University, Film/Video + Communication Studies, class of 2020. Home is a 1915 stucco house in the Village of Caledonia with my wife, our kid, and the dog.
