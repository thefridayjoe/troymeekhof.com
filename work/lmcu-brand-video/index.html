Brand video

Lake Michigan Credit Union

From May 2024 to December 2025 I was the Senior Media Production Specialist at LMCU, one of the largest credit unions in Michigan. My job was to produce brand-approved video and photography for a marketing team of 35-plus people, and to make sure every piece served a campaign goal instead of just looking nice.

The setup

A team of 35 is a different animal from a two-person agency. Every video has a campaign owner, a brand reviewer, a compliance check, and a channel manager waiting on it. The production person sits in the middle of that.

What that meant in practice: I wasn’t just shooting and editing.

What I produced

Social video. Short-form for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Vertical first. I have been saying horizontal is dead for social since 2022, and this job was two years of proof.

TV commercials. Broadcast has a different rulebook than social: legal supers, exact runtimes, safe areas, deliverable specs for each station. Also a different pace. Nobody scrolls past a TV spot, so the first second doesn’t have to scream.

Educational series. Same principle as my Treadstone work: answer the real questions, plainly.

Training content. Internal videos for staff onboarding and process training. Unglamorous, high-volume, and watched by more people than most commercials.

Brand photography. Headshots, branch photography, lifestyle imagery for web and print.

Internal live streams. Multi-camera, live-switched, with an audience that notices every dropped frame. Live is the one format where you can’t fix it in post.

How I worked

  • Audio first, always. Bad audio gets a video killed in review faster than anything else.
  • One look. I built and maintained a set of color and graphics standards so a social clip and a TV spot read as the same brand.
  • Fast turnaround. A 35-person team generates requests constantly. Most of my value was in being reliable, not brilliant.
  • Compliance-aware. Financial services content has rules, and I already knew them from two years in mortgage.

Result

What I took from it: producing inside a large organization is about process as much as craft. The best shot in the world is useless if it misses the brand review. That experience is what lets Grand Rapids Marketing Co. work with bigger clients without slowing them down.

If you have an in-house team that needs more production capacity, or a brand that needs a consistent video look across every channel, this is the work. Get in touch.
