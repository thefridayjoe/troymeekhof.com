FAQs

What is a Search Gap Audit?

It’s a free report comparing the searches your competitors rank for against the ones you do. I use Semrush and Search Console data to find the terms with real buyer intent that you’re missing, then rank them by how much traffic is on the table. It takes a few days and there’s no obligation.

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO gets you ranked in traditional search results. GEO, generative engine optimization, gets you cited correctly by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. They share a foundation of clear structure and verifiable facts, but GEO puts extra weight on entity data, schema, and being the obvious source of truth about your business.

Do you work outside Grand Rapids?

Yes. GRMC’s core clients are West Michigan businesses, but SEO and web work don’t care about geography, and I’ve shot video projects as far away as California. If you’re local, I’ll probably show up in a Cybertruck.
